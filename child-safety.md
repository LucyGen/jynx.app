## Our Commitment to Child Safety

Jynx is a gaming social platform that connects gamers through algorithmic matchmaking, community chat, and game sessions. The safety of minors on our platform is our highest priority. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, or any form of child exploitation.

**Users are strictly prohibited from creating, uploading, sharing, searching for, or distributing CSAM on Jynx.** Violations result in immediate permanent ban and may be reported to relevant authorities.

This page describes the measures we take to protect children and young people who use Jynx, and how we respond to threats to their safety.

## Minimum Age

Users must be **at least 18 years old** to create a Jynx account, and Jynx is rated **18+** on both the Apple App Store and Google Play. Users are required to declare their date of birth during account creation — accounts cannot be created without confirming this minimum age requirement.

This 18+ threshold surpasses the minimums set by:

- **GDPR Article 8** (EU): which sets a 16 ceiling for parental-consent information-society services
- **COPPA** (US): which requires a 13+ minimum

Users determined to be minors are removed from Jynx: accounts identified as belonging to users under 18 are banned by our moderation team. Parents or guardians who believe a minor under 18 has created an account may contact us at **support@jynx.app** to request immediate account removal.

## Content Moderation

### Automated Systems

Jynx employs multiple layers of automated content moderation:

- **Profanity and banned word filter** — A 500+ word list (based on LDNOOBW) with obfuscation detection (spaces, asterisks, unicode lookalikes, zero-width characters) filters user-generated content in real time.
- **Auto-escalation** — Reports categorized as harassment or hate speech are automatically flagged as high priority. Content receiving 3+ reports from distinct users is auto-escalated for urgent review.
- **Profile picture auto-hide** — Profile pictures receiving 3+ reports are automatically hidden from public view pending moderator review.
- **ML image classification** — Uploaded profile pictures and gallery photos are automatically analyzed by machine learning for inappropriate content; flagged images are queued for moderator review.
- **Known-CSAM hash scanning** — Images shared in direct messages are analyzed by automated systems that compare irreversible hashes derived from those images against databases of known child sexual abuse material. For this analysis the images themselves are never transmitted to third parties — only hashes are, matched by specialized child-safety technology providers in the EU region (see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) §4.7 and §5.8).

### Human Moderation

- **Moderator review queue** — All flagged content enters a review queue accessible by trained moderators.
- **Admin dashboard** — Moderators and administrators can review reports, take enforcement actions (warning, mute, temporary ban, permanent ban), and track moderation statistics.
- **Incident escalation** — Critical child safety incidents are escalated immediately to designated staff members.

## Reporting

### In-App Reporting

Every Jynx user can report inappropriate content directly from:

- **Chat messages** — Any message in community chat, private chat, or game session chat
- **User profiles** — Profile picture, bio, or display name
- **Community content** — Community name or description

Reports can be filed under the following categories:

- **Child Safety** — Content or behavior that endangers, exploits, or sexualizes minors — reports are treated as highest priority and reviewed immediately
- **Harassment** — Targeted intimidation, bullying, or abuse
- **Hate Speech** — Content attacking protected characteristics
- **Inappropriate Content** — Sexual, violent, or otherwise unsuitable content
- **Spam** — Repetitive, unsolicited, or promotional content
- **Other** — Any content that violates our policies but doesn't fit above categories

### Report Handling

1. Child safety and hate speech reports are **automatically escalated to high priority**.
2. Content with 3+ reports from distinct users is **auto-escalated**.

## User Blocking

Users can block anyone at any time. When a user is blocked:

- The blocked user is removed from the blocker's match queue
- All chat requests between the two users are deleted
- Existing private conversations and messages are deleted
- The blocked user cannot send new messages or requests

## Real-Time Voice Rooms

Jynx offers real-time voice rooms where users can talk with each other while playing. Voice rooms are a real-time voice surface: audio is transmitted live between the participants in a room and is never recorded or transcribed by Jynx or by our voice infrastructure provider (see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) §3.6).

Safety mitigations that apply to voice rooms include:

- **Per-room capacity cap** — each voice room has a maximum number of participants, keeping conversations manageable and ensuring a blocked participant can be removed from a small, known group.
- **Instant block-sever** — blocking a user immediately ends that user's participation in any voice room you are in, so blocking is an effective real-time safety tool, not just a profile-level filter.
- **No recording or transcription** — voice-room audio is not recorded or transcribed by Jynx or by our voice infrastructure provider, so there is no archived voice content to misuse.

**Known limitation:** Jynx does not perform real-time moderation of voice audio. Because live audio is not recorded, it cannot be reviewed after the fact either. Users who experience harassment, threats, or other misconduct in a voice room should **leave the room**, **block the offending user** (which severs them from your voice room instantly), and **report** the incident through the in-app reporting tools described above. Reports are reviewed under the timelines set out in the Enforcement section below.

## Enforcement

Violations of our content policy result in graduated enforcement actions:

- **Tier 1:** Warning — Permanent record
- **Tier 2:** Temporary Mute — 6–24 hours
- **Tier 3:** Temporary Ban — 3–14 days
- **Tier 4:** Permanent Ban — Indefinite
- **Tier 5:** Permanent Ban + Legal Referral — Indefinite + law enforcement

**Child exploitation offenses (CP-01):** Any involvement with CSAM or grooming results in **immediate permanent ban**. Jynx may report such incidents to NCMEC and/or relevant law enforcement authorities where required or appropriate under applicable law. There are no exceptions and no appeals for CSAM-related enforcement.

Jynx reserves the right to immediately terminate accounts involved in child exploitation or severe safety violations.

## CSAM Reporting to Authorities

Jynx is committed to reporting child sexual abuse material to the appropriate authorities where required or appropriate under applicable law:

- **United States:** Reports may be filed with the **National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline** at [report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org).
- **European Union:** Reports are directed to national hotlines in compliance with Directive 2011/93/EU (for example, the Pharos platform of the French Point de Contact).
- **Brazil:** Reports may be filed with **SaferNet Brasil**, the Brazilian hotline for online child sexual abuse and other crimes against human rights on the internet, at [safernet.org.br](https://www.safernet.org.br).
- **United Kingdom (international):** Reports may be filed with the **Internet Watch Foundation (IWF)**, the UK hotline, at [report.iwf.org.uk](https://report.iwf.org.uk).
- **International:** NCMEC serves as the international clearinghouse, forwarding reports to relevant national authorities via INTERPOL channels.

### CSAM Response Protocol

When CSAM is discovered on Jynx, we act swiftly to contain, escalate, preserve, and report:

1. **STOP** — Content is not downloaded, screenshotted, or shared.
2. **CONTAIN** — Content is hidden from public view and the user's account is suspended.
3. **ESCALATE** — Designated staff members are notified immediately.
4. **PRESERVE** — All relevant user data is placed under legal hold for evidence preservation.
5. **REPORT** — A report is filed with the NCMEC CyberTipline as required by law.
6. **DOCUMENT** — A complete incident record is created and securely stored.

## Data Privacy for Minors

- Jynx does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.
- Profile data for users identified as minors is subject to enhanced protections.
- All user data is processed in compliance with GDPR (EU), COPPA (US), and applicable local privacy laws.
- Data retention follows strict schedules with automatic deletion. See our [Privacy Policy](/privacy.html) for details.
- Content involved in safety incidents is preserved under legal hold, separate from routine data retention.

## What We Do Not Tolerate

The following content and behaviors are strictly prohibited on Jynx, with specific attention to the protection of minors:

- **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM)** — Any content depicting, describing, or promoting sexual abuse or exploitation of minors. *Policy Code: CP-01 — Permanent Ban + Legal Referral*
- **Grooming** — Building trust with a minor with intent of sexual exploitation. *Policy Code: CP-01 — Permanent Ban + Legal Referral*
- **Sexual content** — Explicit sexual content including descriptions, images, or links. *Policy Code: CP-09 — Temporary Ban (3–14 days)*
- **Hate speech** — Content attacking protected characteristics (race, religion, sexual orientation, gender identity, etc.). *Policy Code: CP-02 — Temporary Ban (3–14 days)*
- **Harassment and bullying** — Targeted intimidation, degradation, or distress of a specific person. *Policy Code: CP-03 — Temporary Mute to Permanent Ban*
- **Violence and threats** — Content depicting, promoting, or threatening real-world violence. *Policy Code: CP-10 — Temporary Ban to Legal Referral*
- **Doxxing** — Sharing private, personally identifiable information without consent. *Policy Code: CP-05 — Temporary Ban (14 days) to Permanent Ban*

For the complete content policy, see our [Terms of Service](/terms.html).
