Why a purpose-built duo finder

Destiny 2 is a Cooperative looter shooter with PvE raids/dungeons and PvP Crucible/Trials where the queue layer alone won't fix the matchmaking problem. Three game-specific reasons:

  • There is no 'rank' the way other games have rank — Destiny 2's matching signals are Triumph score (PvE prestige), Trials card (PvP), raid completion count, and seal/title progress. Your fireteam finder must surface ALL of these.
  • Raid mechanic knowledge is a hard prerequisite — a 'sherpa' (teacher) raid takes 3-4x longer than an experienced run. LFG must let players tag 'sherpa-friendly' vs 'KWTD' (know-what-to-do).
  • Class/subclass diversity matters for raid DPS phases — three Warlocks all running Well of Radiance vs one of each class running synergistic supers makes a 50% DPS difference.

Jynx matches Destiny 2 players on the variables that the in-game queue and stat trackers ignore: voice intent, schedule overlap, role/operator/legend/champion pool, and current-split verified rank.

Ranks

Match within the platform-enforced gap window. Ranks below.

  • Power Level / Light Level (PvE gear progression, soft-capped per season)
  • Trials of Osiris card: 0-7 wins, Flawless ticket = 7-0
  • Crucible Glory rank: Guardian I-III, Brave I-III, Heroic I-III, Fabled I-III, Mythic I-III, Legend
  • Crucible Valor rank: Guardian-Legend (no losses, just participation)
  • Triumph score (cosmetic prestige)

Rank-gap rule: Destiny 2 doesn't enforce traditional rank-gap rules because most modes scale to a 'power floor' — gear power above the floor doesn't matter in raids and Trials. The functional gap rule is RAID EXPERIENCE: a Day-1 raider and a fresh Light don't belong in the same raid LFG without explicit teach intent. Trials uses card-based matchmaking — your card determines your opponents.

Roles

Match by complementary roles, not duplicate ones.

  • Titan — Tanky frontline. Subclasses: Solar (Sunbreaker), Arc (Striker), Void (Sentinel), Stasis (Behemoth), Strand (Berserker), Prismatic.
  • Hunter — Mobile/DPS-focused. Subclasses: Solar (Gunslinger), Arc (Arcstrider), Void (Nightstalker), Stasis (Revenant), Strand (Threadrunner), Prismatic.
  • Warlock — Support/spellcaster. Subclasses: Solar (Dawnblade/Well of Radiance), Arc (Stormcaller), Void (Voidwalker), Stasis (Shadebinder), Strand (Broodweaver), Prismatic.

Queue types

  • Raid (6-player fireteam) — The flagship endgame PvE. Each raid (Vow of the Disciple, King's Fall, Crota's End, Salvation's Edge, etc.) has 4-5 encounters with mechanics, DPS phases, and add-clear roles.
  • Dungeon (3-player fireteam) — Smaller raid-style content for 3 players — perfect for trio LFG. Recent: Warlord's Ruin, Vesper's Host, Sundered Doctrine.
  • Trials of Osiris (3v3 PvP) — Sweatiest PvP in the game. Win 7 matches without a loss = Flawless ticket = Lighthouse rewards. Card-based, not season-based.
  • Iron Banner — Quarterly 6v6 PvP event with power-enabled gear. Casual-competitive.
  • Vanguard Ops / Strikes — 3-player matchmade PvE. The starter LFG mode for new fireteams.
  • Onslaught — 3-player horde mode added in 2024. Build-defense gameplay.

How to find a Destiny 2 fireteam without the dreaded LFG ghost

  1. Tag KWTD vs sherpa-friendly explicitly — The single biggest LFG mismatch in Destiny 2 is a 'KWTD only' raid post that admits a fresh raider. Both sides leave frustrated. Your LFG profile should let teammates tag this clearly per-raid.
  2. Match by activity, not by hour — A raid LFG and a Trials LFG are different worlds. Don't bundle 'PvP-curious raider' with 'Lighthouse-grinder' in the same fireteam — they want different things. Filter by specific activity intent.
  3. Class composition in raids matters more than power — Two Hunters and two Warlocks running Well + Bait-and-Switch will out-DPS three Titans every encounter. List your class + subclass on your LFG profile so the fireteam can plan supers.
  4. Schedule is everything for raid clears — Raids are 1.5-3 hours. A fireteam that can sync that block weekly clears; one that can't doesn't. The100.io got famous solely for scheduling LFG. Match for time-window overlap before anything else.

Other LFG tools

Where LFG happens today, and what each tool optimizes for:

  • Destiny Trials Report — PvP-specific stat tracking — Trials win rates, K/D, weapon meta. The canonical PvP LFG-adjacent tool.
  • LFG.net (formerly The100.io) — Schedule-based fireteam finder. Players post 'Saturday 8pm raid' and others sign up. The OG Destiny LFG.
  • Bungie official in-game Fireteam Finder — Added 2024. Built into the game — searchable fireteam listings. Light filtering, no scheduling, no voice verification.
  • Destiny 2 LFG Discord servers — Where the bulk of D2 LFG happens. Per-activity channels (raids, dungeons, Trials). Heavy moderation.

Frequently asked questions

Does Destiny 2 have a duo mode or is it all fireteam?
Most Destiny 2 endgame is fireteam (3 or 6). The closest to 'duo' play is Crucible 6v6 modes where two friends queue together against random other duos plus solos. Trials of Osiris is 3v3, so finding a stable fireteam-of-three is more valuable than finding one duo partner. Use 'duo finder' to find your first stable partner, then accrete the third for dungeons/Trials and three more for raids.
What does 'KWTD' mean on Destiny 2 LFG?
Know What To Do — meaning the player has cleared this raid before, knows the mechanics, and won't need a teach. The opposite is 'sherpa-friendly' (teaching welcome). Mismatching these two is the #1 LFG complaint in r/raidsecrets. Your fireteam finder should treat KWTD/sherpa as a hard filter, not a vibe.
Is the in-game Fireteam Finder good enough?
It's serviceable for matchmade content (strikes, Vanguard Ops, Onslaught) and small dungeons. For raids it lacks scheduling, voice verification, and class-comp matching — most active raid groups still use the100.io or a Discord server alongside it. A purpose-built D2 LFG with class/subclass + KWTD + scheduling beats the in-game tool for endgame.
