Duo-Queue Success Rates by Rank Tier: How Much Does Duo Actually Help?

Win-rate uplift from duoing vs solo-queueing across rank tiers — using public Riot match data where available and Jynx app-internal anonymised matching outcomes where they are not.

Last updated May 8, 2026 Published May 8, 2026 Author: Jynx Research

Key findings

  • Duo-queue uplift is real but modest — published academic and op.gg-aggregated data consistently show a 2-5 percentage-point win-rate increase when two skilled players queue together vs solo queueing in League of Legends and Valorant.

Methodology

Duo-queue win-rate uplift is the difference between a player's solo-queue win rate and their duo-queue win rate, expressed in percentage points. Public sources for this metric are limited because Riot Games does not segment player win-rate disclosure by party size in their public data dashboards. Where public data exists (e.g., op.gg's aggregated rank-distribution dashboards, Riot's published season-end blog posts), we cite it directly. For metrics that require party-size segmentation — specifically the per-tier duo uplift figures in the chart — we use Jynx's app-internal anonymised matching outcomes (consenting users, aggregated, no PII).
