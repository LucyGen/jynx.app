And with GTA 6 progression still unannounced, there is no rank to screen with — only heist history.

How to lock a GTA 6 crew before November 19

  1. Screen candidates in today's heists — You cannot test anyone inside GTA 6 yet, but GTA Online's Casino Heist and Cayo Perico expose everything a finale needs: prep discipline, calm when plans collapse, and whether they ghost after a failed run. One heist night is worth ten interview posts on a recruiting board.
  2. Lock platforms before personalities — No PC version is scheduled as of September 2026 and console cross-play is unconfirmed, so platform decides who can physically join your session. Settle PS5, Xbox or PC-later as a crew before anyone gets attached — it is the one mismatch no amount of chemistry fixes.
  3. Assign the four seats early — Classic heists are engineered around a four-player team: the leader who fronts costs, the wheelman, the gunner, the hacker. Handing out seats now makes launch week a rehearsal instead of a negotiation, and everyone learns the seat's habits while the stakes are still low.
  4. Fix the weekly night now — GTA Online has refreshed on a weekly basis for over a decade, and the same rhythm will govern Vice City. A crew with a standing Thursday session catches every bonus window; a crew that plays by mood misses half of them and dissolves by February.
  5. Write the cut-split rule down — The leader-pays-first design makes money the natural first fight. Agree the split before your first finale together — leader recovers setup costs first, then even shares — and the rule survives into GTA 6 whatever its heists turn out to pay.
