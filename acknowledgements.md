# Acknowledgements

Last updated: August 13, 2026

## Thank You

Jynx is built by a very small team. When someone outside that team takes the time to make the platform better — by reporting a security issue, flagging a bug, improving a translation, or contributing in any other way — we think that deserves to be said out loud and in public.

This page is where we say it. It is permanent, and it is the standing offer: if you contribute something we act on, we will credit you here, with the name or handle you choose.

## How to Be Credited

1. **Report it.** Security issues go to [security@jynx.app](mailto:security@jynx.app) — please read the [disclosure policy](/security.html) first, particularly the out-of-scope list. Everything else goes to [support@jynx.app](mailto:support@jynx.app).
2. **Tell us how you want to appear.** Your real name, a handle, or an organisation — whatever you prefer. Include a link (personal site, profile) if you would like one, or tell us you would rather have none.
3. **We ship the fix.** Entries go up after the change is live, not before.
4. **We add you here.** If you later want your entry changed or removed, email us and we will do it.

Credit is described by **contribution category** — for example "DNS & email configuration hardening" — rather than by the technical detail of any individual finding. That keeps the page useful to you as a public record without turning it into a map of past weaknesses.

## What We Do Not Offer

Jynx does not run a paid bug bounty programme and does not pay for reports. Recognition on this page is what we offer, and we offer it sincerely. See the [Security & Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](/security.html) for the full position.
